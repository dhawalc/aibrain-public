# SEO First 10 Days Playbook: Get Indexed Without Writing a Single Blog Post

*Source: r/micro_saas u/Money-Whereas6491 | Saved: 2026-03-20*

---

## The Insight

When launching a SaaS with a brand new domain — zero backlinks, no blog, no authority, no traffic — most advice says "start writing content."

**Don't.** Not yet.

Google can't rank you if it hasn't even noticed you yet. The only goal for the first 10 days: **get crawled, get indexed, and look legit enough to trust.**

---

## Step 1: Fix the Boring Technical Stuff First

Before thinking about traffic, make the site clean and understandable for Google:

- [ ] Submit sitemap to Google Search Console
- [ ] Verify domain property
- [ ] Check for crawl/indexing issues
- [ ] Write proper title tags + meta descriptions
- [ ] Make sure nothing important is blocked in robots.txt
- [ ] Improve load speed (keep it simple, no overkill)

Nothing advanced. Just removing friction.

---

## Step 2: Focus on Distribution Instead of Content

Instead of writing blog posts no one would read (yet), focus on getting the product **placed** across the internet.

Submit to:
- [ ] SaaS directories
- [ ] Startup listing sites
- [ ] Product discovery platforms
- [ ] A few founder communities

**No spam blasting** — just consistent submissions over a few days.

This does two things:
1. Gives Google multiple paths to discover your site
2. Creates initial trust signals

Full list of free submission places: https://www.lauuunch.space/reddit

---

## Results (~30 Days Later)

From just that initial push:

- **Domain Rating: 0 → 12**
- No content engine
- No link building campaigns
- No ads

Just making it easy for Google to find and understand the site early.

---

## Key Takeaways

1. **Technical SEO first** — remove all friction before anything else
2. **Distribution over content** in the early days — get discovered, not published
3. **Consistent directory submissions** — not spam, just steady placement
4. **Trust signals compound** — each listing gives Google another reason to believe you're legit
5. **Content comes AFTER indexing** — write blog posts once Google already knows you exist
