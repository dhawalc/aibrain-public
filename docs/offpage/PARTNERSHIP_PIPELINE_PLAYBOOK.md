# Partnership Pipeline Playbook

## Target Segments
1. Enterprise automation consultants / SIs
2. Niche newsletters and podcasts
3. Community operators in enterprise AI and operations

## Offer Package
- Co-branded checklist or short framework guide
- Joint webinar or discussion session
- Reciprocal distribution post

## Qualification Criteria
- Audience overlap with enterprise operations leaders
- Governance/compliance relevance
- Attributable links and measurable referral quality

## Weekly Cadence
1. Add 10 new targets to `data/offpage/partnership-pipeline.csv`.
2. Send first-touch outreach.
3. Follow up within 3 business days.
4. Close or recycle stale leads every Friday.
