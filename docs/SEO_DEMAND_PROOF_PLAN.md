# SEO Demand Proof Plan

Last updated: 2026-03-09

## Goal
- Show credible pre-MVP market demand using search visibility, qualified visits, and lead capture.
- Build toward thousands of users by concentrating on a few workflow problems with broad search intent.
- Avoid scaled low-value publishing that damages trust, crawl quality, and conversion quality.

## What Counts As Proof
- Non-brand impressions grow every week.
- Specific workflow pages enter top 20 for target queries.
- Organic sessions land on problem pages, not just the homepage.
- Qualified conversions come from those pages and tools.

## Current Baseline
- GA4: 36 sessions, 36 users, 41 views, 1 lead event in the last 28 days.
- Search Console: 3 impressions, 0 clicks.
- Early traffic exists, but SEO proof does not exist yet.

## Strategy
- Build 3 narrow topic wedges around problems teams already search for.
- Within each wedge, publish one flagship page plus a few supporting assets.
- Add one tool or template per wedge that is worth linking to and sharing.
- Use the landing page to route traffic into the best pages, not to carry the full SEO burden.

## Wedges
1. Approval workflows
- Why: already showing early signal in Search Console.
- Buyer pain: how to automate approvals without losing control, auditability, or SLA.

2. Task routing and orchestration
- Why: broad operational surface area across IT, finance, support, and shared services.
- Buyer pain: getting the right work to the right queue, system, or human at the right time.

3. Governance and risk controls
- Why: strong executive and compliance interest, especially for pre-MVP trust building.
- Buyer pain: defining where agents can act autonomously and where human review is required.

## Tools To Build
1. `/tools/approval-workflow-roi-calculator`
- Purpose: capture high-intent visitors who want a quantified business case
- Inputs: current approval volume, average handling time, exception rate, approver count
- Outputs: hours saved, cycle-time reduction, estimated SLA impact
- Conversion move: email or meeting gate for saved result

2. `/tools/agent-governance-risk-matrix`
- Purpose: give teams a concrete way to decide what agents can do autonomously
- Inputs: workflow type, system touched, financial exposure, customer impact, security impact
- Outputs: recommended risk tier, approval pattern, audit requirements
- Conversion move: request a tailored governance review

## Internal Linking Model
- The landing page links to the 3 flagship guides.
- Each flagship guide links to 2-3 supporting pages and 1 tool.
- Each tool links back to the flagship guide and to the main CTA page.
- The blog index features only the current flagship pages and recent supporting assets.

## Distribution Plan
1. Publish one flagship guide first, then distribute it for links and clicks.
2. Turn each guide into:
- one founder LinkedIn post
- one operator-focused thread
- one checklist post with a direct link back
3. Ask 5-10 friendly operators or consultants to review or share the tools.
4. Promote tools before generic articles because they attract more links.

## Measurement
- Weekly dashboard:
- non-brand impressions
- organic clicks
- top queries entering top 20
- organic landing pages
- engaged sessions by page
- lead events by page
- tool completion rate

## What Not To Do
- Do not republish dozens of thin variants of the same topic.
- Do not chase broad AI trend terms before winning workflow-specific queries.
- Do not judge success on total sessions alone while direct traffic dominates.
- Do not add more than one new wedge before the first wedge shows real pull.
